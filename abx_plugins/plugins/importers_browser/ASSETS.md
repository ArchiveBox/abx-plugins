# Setup assets

- `icons/`: brand SVGs from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/develop/icons), distributed under CC0. Brand names and marks belong to their owners.
- `setup/extension-setup.png`: the ArchiveBox browser extension's `docs/site/screenshots/configuration-desktop.png`.
- `setup/persona-sync.png`: the extension's `docs/site/screenshots/cookies-desktop.png`.
- Both screenshots come from the extension's disposable demo profile, captured by `scripts/capture-screenshots.mjs`. They contain no personal accounts or cookie values.

Feed declarations own their icon and setup paths. ArchiveBox serves these bundled assets without contacting the brand's servers.
