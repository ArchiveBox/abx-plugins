Verify publication

Download the proof and manifest, then open OpenTimestamps Stamp & Verify. Select hashes.json.ots and then hashes.json to check the timestamp and its Bitcoin attestation. A pending proof is not yet a confirmed Bitcoin timestamp.

Upgrade and verify later

Keep both files together. After Bitcoin confirmation, upgrade and verify with the OpenTimestamps client:

ots upgrade hashes.json.ots
ots verify hashes.json.ots
